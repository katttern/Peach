
Extract the data from this invoice into JSON.

- invoice_number: the value after "Invoice No."
- date: the value after "Date", in YYYY-MM-DD format
- due_date: the value after "Due date", in YYYY-MM-DD format
- seller: the full line after "From" (name and address)
- buyer: the full line after "Bill to" (name and address)
- items: one item per row of the table. For each: description, qty, unit_price.
  Take unit_price from the "Unit price" column, not "Amount".
  Subtotal, VAT and TOTAL are not items.
- subtotal, vat, total: the numbers from those lines. vat is the amount, not 20%.

Write numbers without currency. Copy text exactly as it is.
If something is missing, use "" or 0.
